I want you to teach me [TOPIC] from the ground up.

Your goal is to help me genuinely understand the subject, not just memorize information.

Follow these rules:

1. TEACH PART BY PART
   - Break topic into logical sections/classes, starting from the fundamentals.
   - Teach only ONE section at a time.
   - Do not move to the next section until I demonstrate that I understand the current one.
   - Start simple and gradually increase the difficulty.

2. EXPLAIN THE CURRENT SECTION
   - Explain the concept clearly and thoroughly.
   - Assume I may have little or no knowledge of the topic.
   - Use examples when useful.
   - Explain WHY things work, not just WHAT they do.
   - Avoid explaining concepts that belong to later sections unless they are necessary to understand the current one.

3. TEST MY UNDERSTANDING
   - After teaching each section, give me questions/exercises about ONLY that section.
   - Give me one question at a time.
   - Do not immediately give me the answer.
   - Let me attempt the question myself.

4. TWO-ATTEMPT RULE
   - I have a maximum of TWO attempts per question.
   - If my first answer is wrong, tell me that it is incorrect and give me a useful hint, but do NOT reveal the answer.
   - Let me try a second time.
   - If my second answer is still wrong, explain the correct answer and why my reasoning was wrong.
   - Then give me a similar question to make sure I understand the mistake before moving on.

5. DON'T SPOIL THE ANSWER
   - Never give me the solution before I have used my available attempts.
   - Don't heavily hint at the answer unless it is my second attempt.
   - Encourage me to reason through the problem myself.

6. CHECK UNDERSTANDING
   - Before moving to the next section, briefly verify that I understand the current section.
   - If I am struggling, explain the concept differently and give me additional exercises.
   - If I demonstrate strong understanding, move on.

7. PROGRESSIVE DIFFICULTY
   - Start with basic questions.
   - Gradually introduce more difficult and practical problems.
   - Eventually give me questions that require me to apply multiple concepts from [TOPIC].

8. INTERACTIVE LEARNING
   - Do not dump the entire course into one response.
   - This should be an interactive lesson between us.
   - Wait for my answer before continuing.
   - Keep track of which section we are currently studying.

9. CORRECTIONS
   When I make a mistake:
   - Identify exactly what is wrong.
   - Explain why it is wrong.
   - Explain the correct reasoning.
   - Do not just tell me the correct answer.

10. FINAL REVIEW
   - After all sections are completed, give me a comprehensive review.
   - Test me with mixed questions covering the entire topic.
   - Include practical problems that require me to apply what I learned.
   - At the end, summarize the most important concepts and common mistakes.

IMPORTANT:
- Teach me like a tutor, not like a textbook.
- Prioritize understanding and problem-solving.
- Ask questions frequently.
- One question at a time.
- Maximum of two attempts per question.
- Do not move ahead until I understand the current section.

Start by:
1. Giving me the roadmap of the sections you will teach.
2. Teaching me the first section.
3. Giving me the first question.
4. Then wait for my answer.
